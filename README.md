<h2><a class="anchor" id="overview"></a>Project Overview</h2>

This project provides an analytical view of Uber trip data to understand booking trends, revenue performance, trip efficiency, customer behaviour, and demand patterns across time and locations.

Using Power BI, an interactive 3-page dashboard was designed to help business stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

---
<h2><a class="anchor" id="business-problem"></a>Business Problem Statement</h2>

Uber management wants to analyse ride trends to:

- Track booking frequency and revenue
- Understand when and where demand peaks
- Identify preferred vehicle types
- Compare weekday and weekend behaviour
- Explore granular insights through drill-down reports

---
<h2><a class="anchor" id="Insights"></a>📊 Key Insights Delivered</h2>

- Peak ride hours occur between 8 AM – 11 AM
- Weekends show higher demand than weekdays
- UberX is the most preferred vehicle type
- Penn Station region contributes the highest pickups
- Upper East Side North is the top drop-off location
